## Wiki Layers

This app is a topic wiki. Respect the layers:

- **`inbox/`** — drop zone. Scan and process dumped files, moving raw material to `raw/` and originals to `inbox/.processed/`.
- **`raw/`** — immutable sources (interviews/, research/, feedback/, reference/, notes/). Never edit after ingestion.
- **`knowledge/`** — compiled articles (concepts/, topics/, references/). Synthesize, don't copy. Every article has frontmatter: `category`, `confidence`, `sources`, `aliases`.
- **`inventory/`** — durable tracking (ideas/, candidates/, entities/). Not factual evidence.
- **`prds/`** — output artifacts `YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`. See [PRD-TEMPLATE.md](prds/PRD-TEMPLATE.md).
- **`domains/`** — bounded sub-problems, each with its own `AGENTS.md` + `knowledge/`.

**Dual-linking**: every cross-reference uses both formats: `[[slug|Name]]` ([Name](../knowledge/topics/slug.md)).
